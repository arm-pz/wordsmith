# Wordsmith example

## User request

Write three short taglines for a small direct-trade coffee roaster. The brand should feel calm and premium, not flashy. Do not claim anything about taste notes or sourcing practices beyond what is in the brief.

## Example response

- Coffee, chosen at the source.
- Good coffee. No grand performance.
- From the people who grow it.

## Why this is a good response

- The options use different ideas and rhythms.
- The voice is restrained rather than loud.
- The copy uses the direct-trade detail in the brief.
- It does not invent flavors, locations, or sourcing claims.
